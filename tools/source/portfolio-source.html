The story

I taught myself SEO in 2016.

Hi, I'm Anthony Jackson. Welcome to my site. Around 2016 my brother told me something that stuck: college isn't everything, go learn a real skill. So I did. Online SEO courses first, then freelance clients who were willing to take a chance on me. Watching a page I worked on climb Google for the first time hooked me permanently.

Freelancing led to Level 10 Contractor, where I started as a strategist and moved up to Head of SEO Operations by 2020, managing a team of writers and owning all of the client-facing work. I kept taking freelance clients the whole time.

In November 2021 I joined GR0, and I've been here ever since. Strategist first. Then Campaign Manager, taking on more client-facing work and bigger campaigns, the ones that became GR0's published case studies. Senior Campaign Manager in December 2025. And in July 2026, Associate Director of SEO & AI Search. I now lead a team of three Senior Campaign Managers, and AI search is my focal point, because that's where search is being rebuilt in real time.

The through line: I like being where search is changing, and turning that change into numbers a client can put in front of their CEO.

Why it worked

The anchors in earned placements reinforced both the head terms and their semantic variations, which helped Google understand topical relevance and lifted core and secondary keywords together. That same authority paid off in AI answers. Large language models lean heavily on traditional SEO signals, so a stronger keyword-rich link profile meant more LLM-driven traffic to both pages. Link building still underpins performance in search engines and generative AI alike.

"By zeroing in on our most competitive collection pages and executing a smart link-building strategy, they helped us move from middle-of-the-pack rankings to dominating page one." Mac Duggal
